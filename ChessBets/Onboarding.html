Step 1: Welcome & Incentives

Welcome to the Chess Betting Experiment! You will be making choices based on historical World Chess Championship matches.

Your earnings are measured in Points. Every Point you earn will be converted into one lottery ticket for a final cash prize drawing at the end of the study.

How do points turn into money? (Get more info)

The Lottery System: Points as Tickets

This experiment uses a performance-based incentive. Instead of a fixed payment, your payout depends on the number of points you earn. Think of every Point as one physical lottery ticket with your name on it.

  • If you earn 4000 Points, you have a much higher probability of being drawn for the prize than if you earn 200 Points.

Important: At the very end of the experiment, the computer will pick one real round at random. From that round, it will pick one of your 10 decisions at random. This specific choice determines your final ticket count. You should treat every Game Number as the only one that counts!

The experiment consists of:

  • This Onboarding: Instructions and verification quizzes.
  • 1 Practice Round: To get used to the interface (no tickets earned).
  • 11 Real Rounds: Where you earn points based on historical outcomes.

Step 2: Understanding Elo Ratings

In every round, we show you the Elo Ratings of two players. Elo is a standard measure of skill in chess.

Basic Rule: A higher Elo player is more likely to win than a lower Elo player.

How does the Elo math work? (Get more info)

The Statistical Logic of Elo

The Elo system calculates the expected score of a player based on their rating relative to their opponent. A difference of 100 points means the higher-rated player has a 64% win probability.

Note on Draws: Our "Black / Tie" option is a collective outcome. You win points if the Black player wins OR if the game is a draw. This is why the win probability for "Black / Tie" is always 100% minus the White player's win probability.

Moneyline Math: Profit + Stake

The Moneyline format focus on how much profit you earn relative to a base of 100 Points.

1. Positive Odds (+): Betting on the Underdog
Formula: (Bet / 100) * Odds + Bet
Example: +300 odds. You bet 200 Points. Profit = (200 / 100) * 300 = 600. Total = 600 profit + 200 stake = 800 Points.
2. Negative Odds (-): Betting on the Favorite
Formula: (Bet / |Odds|) * 100 + Bet
Example: -400 odds. You bet 200 Points. Profit = (200 / 400) * 100 = 50. Total = 50 profit + 200 stake = 250 Points.
{{ else }}
Decimal Math: Odds × Stake

The Decimal format represents the total return (your stake plus your profit) for every 1 Point you bet.

Formula: Bet * Odds
Example 1: Odds are 4.0. You bet 200 Points. Calculation: 200 * 4.0 = 800 Points.
Example 2: Odds are 1.5. You bet 200 Points. Calculation: 200 * 1.5 = 300 Points.
{{ endif }}
